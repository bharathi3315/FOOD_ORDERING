1. Introduction

The Food Ordering and Delivery Platform is an online system that allows customers to browse restaurants, view menus, place food orders, make payments, and track deliveries. The platform connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners in a single system. It helps reduce manual work and provides a convenient, fast, and reliable food ordering experience.

2. Features
Customer Registration & Login – Customers can create accounts and securely log in.
Restaurant Management – Restaurants can manage their details and food items.
Menu Management – Customers can view available food items, prices, and categories.
Food Ordering – Customers can select food items and place orders.
Order Management – Restaurants can accept and update order status.
Online Payment – Supports different payment methods and payment status tracking.
Delivery Management – Delivery details and delivery status can be tracked.
Order Tracking – Customers can check the current status of their orders.
Database Management – Stores customer, restaurant, menu, order, payment, and delivery information.
